## Accuracy safeguards

1. Academic and music programmes are presented as structured Creator Hub educational foundations, not as universal college syllabi or degrees.
2. Each expanded programme states prerequisites, learning outcomes, applied projects, assessment policy and reference sources.
3. Creator Hub completion certificates are labelled as internal completion certificates unless a specific external accrediting body is explicitly named.
4. Employer cards contain representative roles and responsibilities; they do not claim that a role is currently vacant.
5. Exact job requirements are deferred to the employer's current official vacancy because requirements vary by country, location, seniority and posting.
6. No fabricated learner reviews are displayed.
7. Contact, enquiry and donation actions do not claim delivery before the user's email/share app actually sends the information.
8. Regulated fields such as nursing, law, driving, cybersecurity and other licensed professions include scope notes directing learners to the applicable authority.
9. Music/vocal material includes safe-practice language and does not assign vocal registers by gender.
10. Current labour-market context is separated from course content and linked to authoritative sources.
