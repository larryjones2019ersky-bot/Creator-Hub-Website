# Curriculum and Career Reference Sources

Updated: 2026-09-27

The expanded Creator Hub catalogue uses public, authoritative or institutionally published material as reference frameworks. It does **not** copy or claim to reproduce every college's official curriculum.

## Scope and accuracy policy

Where a programme is regulated, licensed or safety-critical, Creator Hub material is educational only. Learners must follow current laws, standards, professional requirements and institutional prerequisites in the jurisdiction where they intend to study or work.
